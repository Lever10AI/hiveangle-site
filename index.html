How it works

Three steps. You spend five minutes; we spend about thirty.

STEP 1

Send your rough idea

Pick a target community, describe your idea in a few sentences, list the facts you can back up, and add the angle you'd normally take.

STEP 2

We measure what works there

We pull the community's history, evaluate every post with the same narrow questions, and fit a model on real engagement, not guesses.

STEP 3

You get 12 ranked angles

A one-page report: approaches that have worked there, approaches to avoid, and untested angles worth trying, with your own angles ranked inside each.

Three buckets, not one ranking

Choosing an angle means weighing a safe bet, things to avoid, and a chance to stand out. The report gives you all three.

Proven: has worked here

Approaches with 15+ past posts whose typical engagement beats the community median. Your angles that use them, ranked.

Avoid: has failed here

Approaches that consistently underperform or break the community's rules. Flagged on your own angles when they land here.

Worth trying: untested here

Approaches almost nobody has used in this community, with an honest predicted range and upside. Labelled as experiments.

Jev measures. History decides.

Zero-shot models pick the more engaging post about 50% of the time, near chance. Models trained on real engagement reach 67-72% (arXiv 2403.09702). So HiveAngle never asks an LLM "will this crowd like it?" It asks narrow, checkable questions about thousands of real posts, then learns what actually correlates with engagement in your target community.

Questions

Straight answers, since this tool grades other people's communities.
Does HiveAngle write the post for me?

Never. It suggests angles and ranks approaches; you write in your own voice. That keeps you inside every community's rules about AI-generated text.

Which communities are supported?

Hacker News, Bluesky and Dev.to at launch. Communities are analyzed once and cached, then refreshed every 30 days, so repeat requests return in about a minute.

What do you need from me?

Your rough idea (1-3 sentences), the facts you can back up, your target community, any disclosure (like "I built this"), and optionally the 1-2 angles you'd normally take. Those get scored alongside the 12 we suggest, so you see directly whether the tool beats your intuition.

What happens after I post?

Paste the post URL back to us. We check its results at 24 hours and 7 days, and every real post makes the model sharper for that community.

Who is this for?

Founders about to launch, devtools marketers, and anyone posting for clients who wants evidence for the angle instead of a guess.
